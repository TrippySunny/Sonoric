Sonoric

Standalone multifunctional music player

A free standalone cross-platform player with open source. It works with a local library, syncs it across devices without streaming services, and builds audio-reactive shaders from rhythm, bass, and frequencies.

Download

Why Sonoric

Local files, a live visualizer, and a library you own.

Fully offline

Playback, tags, and the library work without the internet. Your data stays on the device and is available anytime.

Cross-platform

Native on Windows and Linux, with the same library model planned for mobile.

Audio-reactive shaders

GPU shaders read the spectrum and waveform so color, scale, and layout react to the current track.

Common formats

MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, M4A, and related formats open without extra codecs or setup.
